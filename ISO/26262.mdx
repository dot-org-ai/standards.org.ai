# ISO 26262

**ISO 26262** (*Road Vehicles — Functional Safety*) is the automotive adaptation of IEC 61508. Its hazard analysis and risk assessment classifies each hazardous event by **severity × exposure × controllability** and assigns an **ASIL** (Automotive Safety Integrity Level, A–D, plus QM below the floor). The ASIL is assigned from the *unmitigated* hazard — safety mechanisms then become the evidence the assigned level demands, not inputs to the assignment.
