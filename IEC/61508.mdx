# IEC 61508

**IEC 61508** (*Functional Safety of Electrical/Electronic/Programmable Electronic Safety-Related Systems*) is the root standard of the functional-safety family (ISO 26262 is its automotive adaptation). It assigns each safety function a **Safety Integrity Level** (SIL 1–4) from the risk reduction the function must deliver, prescribes a whole-lifecycle discipline (specification through decommissioning), and requires that detected dangerous failures drive the system to a defined **safe state**.
