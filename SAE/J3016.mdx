# SAE J3016

**SAE J3016** (*Taxonomy and Definitions for Terms Related to Driving Automation Systems for On-Road Motor Vehicles*) defines the industry-standard taxonomy for driving automation: six levels (0–5) distinguished by who performs the driving task, under what conditions, and with what fallback.

This page is a **G1 seed**: it registers the standard as a source of record so downstream vocabularies can cite it by stable `$id`. It describes the standard; it does not redefine it.

## Constructs contributed

| J3016 construct | Definition (as in the standard) |
|---|---|
| Levels 0–5 | Graduated ownership of the driving task, from no automation (0) to full automation under all conditions (5). |
| DDT — Dynamic Driving Task | The real-time operational and tactical functions required to operate the vehicle. |
| <a id="ODD"></a>ODD — Operational Design Domain | The operating conditions under which an automation system is specifically designed to function; a Level 4 system is Level 4 *within its ODD*. |
| MRC — Minimal Risk Condition | The stable, reduced-risk state a system achieves when a trip cannot or should not be continued. |
| Remote assistance | Human support that advises the system without becoming the driver. |
