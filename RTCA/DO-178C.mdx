# RTCA DO-178C

**RTCA DO-178C** (*Software Considerations in Airborne Systems and Equipment Certification*) governs certification of airborne software. Each software item is assigned a **Design Assurance Level** (DAL A–E) from the severity of the failure condition it can contribute to — catastrophic through no-safety-effect — and the assigned level determines the certification objectives, the required evidence, and the independence with which that evidence must be produced and verified. Certification credit attaches to the *type design*; conformity of each produced instance is a separate, continuing obligation.
